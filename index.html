DENTAL TIPS

Small habits, healthier smile.

Everyday tips to support your oral health. Ask your dentist for advice tailored to you.

01 · DAILY CARE

Why flossing matters

Flossing cleans between teeth where a toothbrush may not reach. Make it part of your daily routine.

02 · HEALTHY HABITS

Look after your teeth

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and limit frequent sugary snacks and drinks.

03 · YOUR VISIT

Feeling nervous?

Tell your dentist what worries you, ask questions and let them know if you need a break.
